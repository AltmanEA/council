## Overview

This is the central registry of binding architectural decisions. ADR identifiers are assigned here and nowhere else; any document introducing a new decision MUST first register it in this table (the identifiers are globally unique across the project).

ADR-001–005 originate from a multi-role consilium, accepted by consensus of three roles: Architect, Reliability & Security Specialist, Human-Centric Designer. The standalone ADR document referenced by earlier versions is no longer part of the documentation set.

ADR-006–009 originate from `sess-documenter-contract` (2026-11-12); their normative content lives in `../../documenter-service/mvp-contract.md` §5. ADR-010 originates from `sess-doc-reliability-algo` (2026-11-12); its normative content lives in `documenter-service/configuration-guide.md` §Hash Store. ADR-011 originates from `sess-doc-reliability-algo` (2026-11-12), operator decision; its normative context lives in `AGENTS.md` §1.5. ADR-012 originates from `sess-translate-automation` (2026-11-14), operator decisions; its normative content lives in `docs/en/documentation-rules/index.md` §3.3 and the Documentation Worker skill. ADR-013 originates from the prose-synchronization session of 2026-11-14 (operator decisions C-1/E2); it amends ADR-007; its normative content lives in `AGENTS.md` §1.5/§1.7 and `documenter-service/mvp-contract.md` §4. ADR-014 originates from `sess-translation-extraction` (2026-11-15), operator decisions; its normative content lives in `AGENTS.md` §1.6–§1.7 and `docs/en/translation-project/`. ADR-015 originates from `p3-operator-surface-impl-plan` (2026-11-15), operator decisions OD-1–OD-3 confirming moderator recommendations; its normative content lives in `docs/en/operator-ingress/index.md` §pre_check Gate and the session consensus document. ADR-016 originates from `sess-operator-surface-split` (2026-11-17), operator decision (action item AI-4); its normative content is the Tier 2+ Activation Gate Norm adopted in the session consensus document. ADR-017 originates from `sess-solo-operator-activation` (2026-11-17), moderator-confirmed operator decision (stance d, bounded risk experiment); its normative content lives in `documenter-service/mvp-contract.md` §4 and `documenter-service/policy-reference.md` (§Field Reference, §Authorization Workflow step 6). ADR-018 originates from sess-term-operator-surface (2026-11-18), operator decision (consensus of Architect / R&S / HCD); its normative content lives in sessions/sess-term-operator-surface/consensus-doc.md and docs/en/glossary/index.md; it registers the terminology rename Operator Surface → Operator Ingress (RU: "Точка входа оператора"). ADR-019 originates from the same session's changeset D (2026-11-18), operator decision; it amends the sentinel-path citation of ADR-016 and completes ADR-018 by renaming code-level identifiers (sentinel path, OPERATOR_INGRESS_SENTINEL, operator_ingress_ready) during the Tier 2+ interlock-closed window; its normative content lives in docs/en/operator-ingress/index.md §Security and Threat Model.
